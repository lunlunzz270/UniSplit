# Weekly Scrum Meeting Schedule

The UniSplit team will hold weekly Scrum meetings to review progress, discuss blockers, and plan upcoming work for the sprint.

## Meeting Details

**Meeting Type:** Weekly Scrum Meeting    
**Location:** Zoom 

## Purpose

The weekly meeting allows the team to:

- Review completed tasks from the previous week
- Identify blockers or technical issues
- Update the sprint backlog
- Coordinate upcoming development tasks
- Ensure all members remain aligned with sprint goals

## Sprint 1 Meeting

**Date:** Monday, March 16, 2026  
**Time:** 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

## Meeting Structure

Each meeting will briefly cover:

1. What each member completed since the last meeting
2. What each member plans to work on next
3. Any blockers or issues that need team discussion
4. Updates to backlog or sprint tasks
